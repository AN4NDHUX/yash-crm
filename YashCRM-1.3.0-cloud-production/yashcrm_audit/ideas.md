# Yash CRM design brief

## Ground-truth reference

Yash CRM is a functional CRM inspired by the core information architecture and workflow conventions of Zoho CRM: a navigation-led workspace, record modules, pipeline management, related activities, and administration settings. The reference informs capability organization only; Yash CRM uses its own identity and interface treatment.

## Design direction

**Quiet Operations / Salt-and-Pepper Future** — a minimal, high-clarity workspace for teams that need to scan, update, and move customer records quickly, using the uploaded white, light-gray, mid-gray, and charcoal palette.

- **Design movement:** editorially calm enterprise software, with generous white space and crisp information density.
- **Core principles:** hierarchy before decoration, pure contrast for action, strong status language, and shallow visual depth.
- **Color philosophy:** pure white `#FFFFFF`, light gray `#D4D4D4`, mid gray `#B3B3B3`, and charcoal `#2B2B2B`, with semantic status tones used sparingly.
- **Layout paradigm:** fixed navigation rail plus a wide content canvas; data views use compact tables and modular cards; detail views use a summary rail with related activity.
- **Signature elements:** compact white navigation rail, quiet active-nav contrast, small uppercase section labels, thin gray divider lines, rounded 10px controls, a subtle CSS texture, and the monogram mark built from two interlocking angled shapes.
- **Interaction philosophy:** immediate feedback, inline state changes, clear empty states, reversible archive/delete actions, consistent modal forms, and card lift/sheens on pointer hover.
- **Animation:** continuously drifting grain/grid texture plus a slow gloss sweep behind the workspace, paired with restrained glass-card hover transitions; all motion yields to `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Typography system:** Inter/system sans for clarity, 12px labels, 14px body, 18–32px headings with tight line-height.
- **Brand essence:** dependable, focused, quietly capable.
- **Brand voice:** concise, practical, optimistic without hype.
- **Wordmark/logo:** “Yash CRM” with a compact YC monogram; the monogram uses two solid indigo shapes with negative space between them.
- **Signature palette:** salt `#FFFFFF`, pepper-light `#D4D4D4`, pepper-mid `#B3B3B3`, and pepper-dark `#2B2B2B`.
